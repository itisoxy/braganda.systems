The Problem

Reps Engage Blind.

Most reps walk into a call or send an email with a generic pitch, because pulling together real account context — recent news, hiring signals, tech stack, funding — takes longer than the call itself.

GTM Intelligence automates that research layer — monitoring signals, scoring accounts against your ICP, and surfacing a concise brief before every engagement.

How It Works
Signals → Research → Scoring → Intelligence → Action
Step 01

Monitor Signals

Track trigger events — funding, hiring, leadership changes, technology adoption — across target accounts.

Step 02

Research & Score

AI research and ICP scoring turn raw signals into a ranked, prioritised account list.

Step 03

Deliver Intelligence

Company summaries and contact context land in your CRM and sales alerts, ready to act on.
