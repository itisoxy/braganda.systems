The Challenge

Speed Matters.
Manual Doesn't.

Problem

When a new enquiry comes in, speed matters — the odds of properly qualifying a lead fall sharply within the first few minutes of contact. But the typical process still relies on someone manually reviewing the enquiry, judging how valuable it is, keying it into the CRM and drafting a response. That's manageable when enquiries are rare. It breaks down the moment volume increases, an enquiry lands out of hours, or the person responsible is in a meeting, on leave, or simply hasn't checked their inbox yet — every one of those gaps is a lead going cold.

Solution

For Braganda, I wanted to remove that operational gap entirely and built a system around six connected stages that run automatically the moment an enquiry lands, with no person needing to touch it. Capture pulls in the enquiry the instant it's submitted; Qualify uses AI to score it against real commercial criteria; Route applies business rules to decide where it goes next; Update CRM pushes a fully populated record into Zoho; Respond sends the right message back within seconds; and Track logs the entire outcome for visibility.

Benefit

The result isn't just faster response times, though those matter. It's a shift in where human attention goes — instead of spending the first part of every enquiry on data entry and triage, the team only sees a lead once it's already scored, routed and logged, free to spend their time on the conversations that actually move deals forward, while human involvement stays exactly where it matters.

The Solution

Six Steps.
Fully Automated.

An AI-powered Speed-to-Lead system that takes a new Braganda enquiry through the complete first stage of the sales process.

01
Capture

Capture

A new website enquiry triggers the workflow instantly through a webhook.

02
Qualify

Qualify

OpenAI analyses the enquiry against defined commercial criteria and returns a lead score, priority classification and reasoning.

03
Route

Route

n8n applies business rules to the score. High-priority leads go straight to the sales route; others follow the standard sequence. AI provides the intelligence — workflow logic decides what happens next.

04
Update CRM

Update CRM

The lead is automatically created in Zoho CRM, with contact, enquiry and qualification data mapped into the record.

05
Respond

Respond

The appropriate email response is automatically triggered, reducing the time between enquiry and first contact.

06
Track

Track

The lead, score and workflow outcome are logged in Google Sheets, creating visibility into how the automation is performing.
